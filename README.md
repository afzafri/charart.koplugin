# Character Art

A KOReader plugin. Highlight a character's name while reading and see art of
them without leaving the book.

Reading *Dungeon Crawler Carl* and want to know what Princess Donut actually
looks like? Highlight her name, tap **Character art**, and you get the fan art
from the book's wiki — artist credit included.

## Status

Early work in progress.

## Installing

Copy the `charart.koplugin` directory into KOReader's `plugins` directory and
restart KOReader.
